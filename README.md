### Hi, I'm Cong 👋

I work at the intersection of **bioinformatics, medical statistics, and AI-assisted scientific analysis**. I build analysis workflows and tools that connect biological questions with code, interpretable results, and reproducible reports.

My interests also include **AI-assisted protein and antibody design**, especially how sequence and structure models can inform design decisions and how experimental data can help evaluate those decisions.

我关注生物信息学、医学统计与 AI 辅助科研，也在探索 AI 蛋白与抗体设计，关注如何用序列和结构模型辅助设计，并用实验数据评估设计结果。

🧬 **Explore biology** — bulk RNA-seq, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics.<br />
📐 **Make methods explicit** — statistical analysis for antibody and biologics research.<br />
🤖 **Build with agents** — turn analysis workflows into reusable scientific tools.

**Currently building**

**[AgenticPrism](https://github.com/the8thday/AgenticPrism-SKILL)**<br />
Modular analysis skills for antibody and biologics R&D, with saved configurations, diagnostics, and reports. **Public beta for research use**; method-specific validation and limitations are documented in the repository.

**[BioQuorum](https://github.com/the8thday/bioquorum)**<br />
An open biomedical research workbench that works with different AI models. It lets researchers trace claims back to source passages or recorded calculations and export the evidence for review.
